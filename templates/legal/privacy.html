StudyFlow

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

StudyFlow is a free study app for university students. It is an independent project built and run by Afolabi Peter. It is not an official service of any university. This page explains what information the app keeps, who else gets it, and the choices you have.

What we keep

  • Your account: username, email address and password. Your password is stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read it. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Your profile (optional): university, department, level, bio, study goals, preferred study time, avatar colour and a Spotify playlist link.
  • What you do in the app: courses, tasks, timetable, attendance, GPA and CGPA entries, flashcards, study sessions, points and badges, practice-test attempts, library reservations, and your chats with the AI coach (including short notes the coach remembers about you).
  • What you share: files you upload, plus titles, descriptions, ratings, comments, study group posts and chat messages. Other signed-in students can see these, together with your username.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider keeps normal server logs (for example your IP address and the pages requested), and the app uses cookies described below.

Who can see your email

Your email address is shown only to you and to the site administrator. It is not shown to other students.

What we use it for

  • To run the app and show you your own information.
  • To send emails: your confirmation code, password reset links, a welcome message, and an optional daily study email (you can switch it off in your profile).
  • To keep the app safe: limiting abuse, reviewing reported content, and fixing problems.

We do not sell your information and we do not show ads. We did not find any advertising or analytics trackers in the app.

AI features

When you use the AI study coach, or an AI tool on a shared file (summary, quiz, flashcards, questions about a PDF), the text you send is passed to an AI service called Groq to write the answer. For the coach this includes some of your study information, such as your courses, deadlines, tasks and flashcards. For file tools it includes text taken from the file. Please do not type passwords or private details into the coach. AI answers can be wrong, so check important facts. Groq handles that text under its own terms and privacy policy.

Other services that handle your data

  • Render hosts the app and its database (servers in the United States).
  • Brevo delivers our emails, so it sees your email address and the message.
  • Groq processes AI requests, as described above.
  • Google provides Sign in with Google and web fonts. Fonts and some scripts are also loaded from jsDelivr, unpkg and cdnjs, so those providers can see your IP address and browser when a page loads.
  • Spotify, only if you add a playlist: the player is loaded from Spotify.

Cookies and saved settings

  • A session cookie keeps you signed in, and a security (CSRF) cookie protects forms. Both are needed for the app to work.
  • Your theme choice and whether you finished the welcome tour are saved in your browser.
  • Notes you choose to save for offline stay on your device and are cleared when you sign out.
  • We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep your information while your account exists. You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, or delete your account and information. There is no delete button in the app yet, so please contact us (below) and we will do it by hand. Content you shared with others, such as comments, may be removed or made anonymous instead of kept. You can switch off the daily study email in your profile.

Security

The app uses HTTPS, stores passwords hashed, and limits repeated attempts on sensitive actions. No online service is perfectly secure, so use a strong password that you do not use anywhere else.

Who the app is for

StudyFlow is made for university students. It is not meant for young children.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, for big changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests about your information: Afolabi Peter, the developer, on LinkedIn.
