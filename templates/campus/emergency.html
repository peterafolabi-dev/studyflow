Campus Safety Protocols

🏥 Medical Emergency

For urgent clinic ambulance dispatch, call the GK Clinic line first. State your exact hall, block, or lecture theatre clearly.

🛡️ Night Travel & Security

Avoid walking alone from GK Gate to off-campus lodges past 10:00 PM. If you suspect suspicious activity, notify the Main Gate Security immediately.

For student rights disputes or hostel harassment, contact the SUG Welfare Director or the Dean of Student Affairs unit.
